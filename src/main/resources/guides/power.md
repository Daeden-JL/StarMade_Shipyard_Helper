# Power (reactors)

Every system draws power. Power comes from Reactor Power blocks.
- Place Reactor Power blocks touching each other so they form one group. Only the largest group on the ship is the active reactor.
- A reactor needs Reactor Stabilizer blocks to run at full output: roughly as many stabilizers as reactor blocks.
- Stabilizers only work well at a distance from the reactor, and the distance needed grows with reactor size. For small ships put the stabilizers at the opposite end of the hull from the reactor. If stabilization is low the reactor produces far less power.
- Stabilizers do not need to be linked or connected to the reactor.
- Reactor chambers (Reactor Defence Chamber, Reactor Mobility Chamber and others) add abilities. A chamber must be connected to the reactor by a path of Reactor Conduit blocks and must be about a tenth of the reactor's size or more.
- Do not use the block named "Power (Old)".

Sizing rule of thumb: start with reactor blocks equal to about 5 to 10 percent of the ship's total block count, the same number of stabilizers, and adjust when the player reports power problems in the game.

Typical layout: reactor as a compact box low in the middle or rear of the hull, stabilizers as a slab near the nose.
